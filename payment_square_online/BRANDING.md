# Square Online Payments listing assets

The two release branches share their visual design and locally stored assets.
Installation instructions remain specific to each Odoo version. The Store
HTML does not load third-party images or scripts.

## Official Square symbols

- Source: https://design.block.xyz/square/guidelines/logo
- Download: https://design.block.xyz/downloads/square_symbol.zip
- `icon.png`: unmodified black Square symbol, 300 x 300.
- `square-symbol-white.png`: unmodified white Square symbol, 300 x 300.
- Reused from the repository's existing `odoo_square/static/description/` assets.

Square symbols remain property of their owner. The addon license does not grant
rights to Square trademarks. This is an independent integration maintained by Davai.
