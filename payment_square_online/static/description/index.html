How it works

From the cart to the payment record.

01

Choose Square at checkout

The shopper completes their Odoo cart, billing and delivery details, then selects the Square card payment option.

02

Enter and verify the card

The embedded Square form collects card details and requests bank authentication when required.

03

Follow the payment in Odoo

The verified payment is linked to the existing website order and recorded in the configured bank journal. Invoices and deliveries follow Odoo workflows.

Features

A clear payment workflow.

Checkout

Embedded card payments

Offer one-time card payments inside your Odoo eCommerce checkout, with card verification handled by Square.

Your account

Authorize with Square

Connect the intended Square merchant through OAuth. Keep Sandbox and Production configurations separate and renew access through the built-in connection workflow.

Refunds

Full and partial amounts

Authorized accounting users can refund captured payments. Pending refunds reserve the available balance, and confirmed refunds create outgoing Odoo payment records.

Recovery

Review uncertain payments

Use signed Square notifications, scheduled checks and Refresh status to retrieve the outcome of an existing payment or refund.

Currency

Match your Square location

Offer Square when the checkout currency matches the authorized location. Scope the provider to its Odoo company and, optionally, a website.

Channels

Website and POS together

Use this addon on its own, or install the matching Square POS Integration release alongside it. Configure each channel separately and update both addons together.

Payment follow-up

Check the payment.
Keep the original attempt.

A checkout can lose its response while Square is still processing. Open Square Payment History and use Refresh status before allowing another attempt.

Retrieve the outcome

Signed notifications and scheduled checks can update processing payments.

Keep historical access

Past payments and refunds stay tied to their original Square connection and location.

Review changed orders

A payment captured after an order changes or is cancelled is flagged for authorized review.

Compatibility

Designed and tested for self-hosted Odoo 19.0 Community. Odoo Online hosting does not support this Python addon.

An administered Odoo 19.0 Community server with eCommerce, accounting and the declared addon dependencies. Python packages cryptography and requests must be installed.

A Square Developer application and an authorized merchant with an active card-processing location.

A public HTTPS checkout origin, the required Square Content Security Policy and a publicly reachable webhook endpoint.

A bank journal for the provider company, a compatible checkout currency and a server encryption key shared by all Odoo workers.

Supported seller locations

Six processing currencies. Eight seller markets.

Australia (AUD), Canada (CAD), France, Ireland and Spain (EUR), Japan (JPY), the United Kingdom (GBP), and the United States (USD).

The website checkout currency must match the selected Square location. International buyers can use eligible cards, subject to Square and issuer approval.

Setup

Configuration, in four stages.

01

Install and prepare

Install payment_square_online from the Odoo 19.0 release. Configure the company chart of accounts and a bank journal. Set SQUARE_ONLINE_ENCRYPTION_KEY in the server environment before saving Square credentials. Share the same key across workers and back it up privately.

02

Authorize your merchant

Open the Square Online payment provider and choose Manage Square Connections under Credentials. Create a Sandbox connection with its application ID and secret. Register the OAuth Redirect URL shown in Odoo, then use Authorize with Square .

03

Choose location and journal

Select an eligible Square location and a bank journal in the provider company. Assign the website if needed and match its pricelist currency to the Square location. Use Test Mode and publish the provider on your test store.

04

Test, then enable Production

Configure the signed webhook subscription shown in the connection and test a successful payment, a decline, bank authentication and a refund in Sandbox. Create and authorize a separate Production connection, select it on the provider, then enable and publish the provider.

Good to know

Payments, accounting and fulfillment.

Refunds

Keep the accounting complete

A confirmed refund posts an outgoing payment. Create and reconcile any required credit note separately in Accounting, and process goods returns separately in Inventory.

Orders

Use Odoo order workflows

Website invoices follow your invoicing policy. Odoo handles delivery through its usual fulfillment workflow. Cancelling an order leaves any decision to refund with Accounting.

External service and data

What is exchanged with Square?

This addon connects your Odoo website to Square. Review the service and data flows before authorizing your merchant account and enabling checkout.

Card entry and verification

The browser sends card details and the billing details needed for verification to Square through its Web Payments SDK. The addon uses the resulting payment token.

Payments and refunds

The Odoo server sends payment tokens, amounts, currencies, merchant and location identifiers, and order or transaction references to the selected Square environment.

Recorded in Odoo

Encrypted Square connection credentials, payment and refund references, amounts and processing statuses support monitoring and recovery.
