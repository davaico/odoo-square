# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively
- keep technical disagreement focused on facts, tradeoffs, and project goals
- welcome useful contributions from people with different levels of Odoo, Square, Python, and operations experience
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of private information

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants whose behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers privately with the relevant context and links.
