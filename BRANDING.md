# Square listing assets

The sales synchronization and Terminal applications have separate HTML descriptions
and separate cover images. All images are stored locally. No generated image or
invented Odoo screenshot is used.

## Official symbols

- Guidelines: https://design.block.xyz/square/guidelines/logo
- Download: https://design.block.xyz/downloads/square_symbol.zip
- Black `icon.png` and white `square-symbol-white.png`: unmodified, 300 x 300.
- The same exact symbols are included in each addon.

## Sales synchronization: Square Register

- Source page: https://squareup.com/us/en/hardware/register
- Asset: https://images.ctfassets.net/2d5q1td6cyxq/4RVkJmjVdBA8p9cthDU0Bc/dafc275105042df4ca6626caf5758620/Register_X2c_Phone_Tap__-_USEN.png
- File: `odoo_square/static/description/square-register.png`, 1200 x 628.
- Unmodified Square photograph showing a Square Register at checkout. It represents
  a sale made in Square POS before its records are imported into Odoo. This is an
  example of Square hardware, not a requirement to buy this specific register.

## Odoo POS payment: Square Terminal

- Source page: https://squareup.com/fr/fr/hardware/terminal
- Asset: https://images.ctfassets.net/2d5q1td6cyxq/1Lllo3ScaJJ12rIieEAs0A/b8378f8fd8556ba7ba7aa106f6c04d29/PD07466_-_FRFR_Dip_a_chip_card.png
- File: `pos_square_terminal/static/description/square-terminal.png`, 960 x 1200.
- Unmodified Square photograph showing a card inserted into the payment device.
- Reused from the repository's previous listing. Its caption explains that the
  cashier operates Odoo POS while the customer pays on the Terminal.
- The previous copy under `odoo_square` is retained for existing asset references.

Square names, symbols and photographs remain property of their owner. The addon's
AGPL-3 license does not grant rights to them. The pages identify Davai as the
independent maintainer. Earlier-release Odoo screenshots in the importer listing
remain labelled as interface examples.
