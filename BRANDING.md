## Symbols

- Source and usage guidelines: https://design.block.xyz/square/guidelines/logo
- Official download: https://design.block.xyz/downloads/square_symbol.zip
- `icon.png`: `square_symbol/square_symbol_black.png`, 300 x 300.
- `square-symbol-white.png`: `square_symbol/square_symbol_white.png`, 300 x 300.

The header preserves the logo proportions and white color against a dark background.
The black symbol is the addon icon. The page identifies the integration as maintained
by Davai and includes a short trademark attribution.
